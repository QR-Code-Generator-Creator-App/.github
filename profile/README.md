## 🚀 Summary
QR Code Generator for Windows packs everything you need to make scannable codes into one desktop app. Encode a website link, plain text, a contact card, or Wi-Fi credentials, then customize and export the result in a few clicks. Thanks to dynamic QR codes, you can send a printed code to a new destination later without reprinting a thing. From product packaging to event signage and quick digital business cards, this app keeps your QR workflow fast, private, and entirely on your PC.
